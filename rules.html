🥷 SHADOW NINJA

Rules • Controls • How to Play • Weapons • Levels

🎮 How to Play

Shadow Ninja is a fighting adventure where you control your own ninja. Fight enemies, defeat bosses, earn coins, unlock weapons and complete increasingly difficult levels.

  1. Choose your ninja name.
  2. Choose your custom ninja skin.
  3. Select a weapon.
  4. Choose a level or Boss Battle.
  5. Defeat enemies.
  6. Collect coins.
  7. Unlock better weapons.
  8. Complete every level.
  9. Defeat the bosses.
💡 Tip: Don't constantly attack! Blocking and dashing can help you avoid enemy attacks.

⌨️ Controls

W Move Up
A Move Left
S Move Down
D Move Right
J Normal Attack
K Heavy Attack
L Special Attack
I Block
SHIFT Dash
1 - 0 Select Weapons
ESC Return to Menu

📜 Game Rules

  • You must defeat the enemies to complete a level.
  • If your health reaches zero, you lose the battle.
  • Bosses have much more health than normal enemies.
  • Some weapons must be unlocked using coins.
  • You cannot use a locked weapon until you unlock it.
  • Coins can be earned by winning battles.
  • Your unlocked weapons and coins are saved locally on your device.
  • Boss battles become harder as you progress.
  • Do not leave the game while a battle is saving progress.

⚔️ Combat Rules

Normal Attack

Press J to perform your normal attack. This is useful for quick attacks.

Heavy Attack

Press K for a stronger attack. Heavy attacks are slower but deal more damage.

Special Attack

Press L to perform your special attack. Special attacks are powerful and should be used carefully.

Block

Hold I to block incoming attacks.

Dash

Press SHIFT to quickly move away from danger.

🏯 Levels

  1. Training Grounds — Learn the basic controls.
  2. Forest Path — Fight multiple enemies.
  3. Mountain Temple — Stronger enemies appear.
  4. Shadow Fortress — Challenging combat.
  5. Final Dojo — Prepare for the boss.
⭐ Completing levels rewards you with coins and helps unlock new weapons.

👹 Boss Battles

  • Bosses have large health bars.
  • Bosses deal more damage than normal enemies.
  • Bosses can use stronger attacks.
  • Watch their attack pattern before attacking.
  • Use Dash to escape powerful attacks.
  • Use Block when you cannot safely escape.
⚠️ Bosses are supposed to be difficult. Don't worry if you lose your first attempt!

🪙 Coins & Unlocks

You can earn coins by:

  • Winning battles.
  • Defeating enemies.
  • Completing levels.
  • Defeating bosses.

Use your coins to unlock new weapons and other game features.

🎨 Custom Ninja Skins

Create your own ninja by choosing a name and appearance.

  • Choose your ninja's color.
  • Choose your own name.
  • Change your style whenever you want.
  • Your chosen appearance is used during battles.

🏆 Winning & Losing

You Win When:

  • All required enemies are defeated.
  • You defeat the boss in a boss battle.
  • You complete the level objective.

You Lose When:

  • Your health reaches zero.
  • You fail a required battle.

🥷 Ninja Tips

  • Don't stand still during a fight.
  • Use Dash to avoid powerful attacks.
  • Block when an enemy is about to attack.
  • Use fast weapons against quick enemies.
  • Use heavy attacks when you have a safe opening.
  • Save special attacks for difficult enemies.
  • Learn boss attack patterns.
  • Try different weapons to find your favorite.
